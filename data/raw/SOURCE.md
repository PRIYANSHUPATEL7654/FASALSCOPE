# Crop-data provenance

- Repository: [k0rn/India_Agri_Data](https://github.com/k0rn/India_Agri_Data)
- Dataset filename: `agridata.csv` (bundled here as `india_agriculture_cleaned.csv`)
- Maintainer description: cleaned Government of India agriculture data; CC0-1.0.
- Fields: state, district, crop, fiscal year, season, area (hectares), production, and yield.
- This project filters to Gujarat and recalculates yield as production divided by area.
- Gujarat training window in the supplied source: 1998–99 through 2011–12.

The repository is a third-party cleaned mirror, not the government portal itself. The official portals listed in the project README are the preferred source for future updates.
